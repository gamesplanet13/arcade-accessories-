Before you order

Does every console include the same accessories?

No. Review the included-items list for the selected model or setup. PS2 Console Only includes no adapter, cables, controller, memory card or game drive. Complete and Manual setups list the items you select.

Where do I see courier charges?

The checkout shows delivery choices using your destination and order details. Check all charges before submitting. Final packed measurements may need confirmation from the shop.

Is the warranty the same for every item?

Warranty, condition and return or replacement terms depend on the product and selected plan. Check the product page. For PS2 console warranty, physical damage, burns, tampering and broken seals are excluded; accessories have separate terms.

How do I send my PS2 game choices?

Choose an Optional Games pack, then use the PS2 selector with the matching storage. Review your game list and follow the selector's sharing instructions after booking.

Do I need to confirm compatibility?

If you are unsure, contact the shop with your console model and the part you need before ordering.
