BEFORE YOU PRESS PLAY

A few useful answers.

Open PS2 game selector ↗
Can I buy just the PS2 console?

Yes. Select PS2 → Console only. The ₹8,500 option includes no adapter, cable, controller, memory card or game drive.

What is the difference between Top and Optional games?

Top Games uses the listed pack for your chosen PS2 storage. Optional Games lets you submit your preferences using the PS2 game selector after booking. Choose the correct storage in that selector.

Where do I see delivery and payment options?

Continue to the shared checkout. Enter your pincode to see the available courier and payment options and review the final amount before submitting.

Can I combine consoles and accessories?

Yes. Add a setup to this cart, choose another console, or browse accessories. They use the same shared checkout.
